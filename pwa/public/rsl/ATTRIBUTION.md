# Russian Sign Language preview library

Derived from **Slovo: Russian Sign Language Dataset** (Kapitanov et al., SaluteDevices, 2023), https://github.com/ai-forever/slovo, under the dataset's public license with attribution and conditions reserved (a CC BY-SA 4.0 variant). Changes: hand, body-pose and face landmarks were extracted from the original videos with MediaPipe, one clip per word was selected, coordinates were rounded to 3 decimals. Original videos are not included.

This is Russian Sign Language, not Kazakh Sign Language, and has not been reviewed by native signers. Derived data are shared under the same license terms.
